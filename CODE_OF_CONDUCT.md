# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project. Assume good intent, provide technical evidence and make room for contributors with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

Do not use this project or its community spaces to encourage unauthorized load testing, service disruption or credential exposure.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/sacuadros). Do not disclose private details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation or block repeat offenders. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact and repeated behavior.
